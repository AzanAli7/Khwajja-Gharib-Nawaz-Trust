Our Projects

An Integrated Township for Education, Health & Dignity

Founded by Sajjada Nasheen Peer Syed Amir Moin Kazmi, and led by Chairman Sajjada Naseen Peer Syed Adnan Moin Kazmi and General Secretary Peer Syed Rizwan Moin Kazmi, the Trust is dedicated to upholding the timeless values of humanitarian service.

Vision

Our vision is to create a beacon of hope and progress, fostering a holistic environment where education empowers, faith enriches, healthcare heals, and compassion thrives. We are committed to alleviating suffering, promoting knowledge, and providing essential resources to the most vulnerable members of our society. Our work encompasses education, charity, medical supplies, and food provision, all aimed at uplifting communities and nurturing a more just and equitable world.

Project Vision & Objectives

To establish a self-sustaining, integrated township that stands as a model for community development, providing state-of-the-art education, advanced healthcare, spiritual grounding, economic empowerment, and dignified living for all residents.

Key Objectives

  • Provide high-quality education from early childhood to vocational training.
  • Deliver comprehensive and accessible healthcare services.
  • Foster a strong sense of community and spiritual well-being.
  • Offer modern, comfortable, and secure residential facilities for staff.
  • Ensure sustainability through thoughtful infrastructure and green initiatives.

Strategic Zoning for Enhanced Living

Healthcare Complex: Hospital, Diagnostic Lab, and Ambulance Bay for essential services.

Community & Commercial Core: Central Park, Sports Complex, Community Center, Market, and Central Parking.

Education & Spiritual Zone: The educational and spiritual heart of the town, fostering learning and faith, comprising The Modern School of Technology, The Central Big Library, and a Skills Development Institute.

The Modern School of Technology

Design: Multi-story (4–5 floors) earthquake-resistant structure with ample natural light and ventilation.

  • Digital classrooms equipped with smartboards, high-speed internet, and video conferencing facilities, with ergonomic furniture for student comfort.
  • A dedicated STEM/Robotics Lab, advanced computer labs, a 3D printing studio, and a language lab.
  • An indoor auditorium, a dedicated sports ground, a basketball court, and a hygienic cafeteria.
  • A specialized wing for O-Level studies, designed to meet international educational standards.

Khwajja Gharib Nawaz Charitable Hospital

Bed Capacity: 100 beds comprising general wards, private rooms, and specialized units like ICU (Intensive Care Unit) and CCU (Cardiac Care Unit) for critical patients.

  • 24/7 Emergency Department, General Medicine, Pediatrics, Gynecology, Orthopedics, and a fully functional Surgical Department.
  • Two fully equipped operation theaters, ventilators, a dialysis unit, X-Ray, Ultrasound, and a CT-Scan machine.
  • Heavy-duty generators plus an integrated solar panel system for uninterrupted, sustainable power.

Advanced Diagnostic Lab Building

Facility: Khwajja Gharib Nawaz Diagnostic Center — providing accurate and rapid diagnostic services with cutting-edge technology.

  • Fully automated machines for blood tests, pathology, microbiology, and histopathology.
  • Radiology wing equipped with digital X-ray, ultrasound, and CT-scan machines.
  • Designed to international bio-hazard safety standards with digital reporting for quick access to results.

Advanced Ambulance Bay & Parking

Facility: Khwajja Gharib Nawaz Ambulance Service — parking for 20 fully equipped ambulances, strategically located near the main entrance for immediate deployment.

  • 5 Advanced Life Support (ALS) ambulances capable of minor operations and stabilization procedures.
  • 15 Basic Life Support (BLS) ambulances.
  • A dedicated workshop for maintenance and repair, plus a rest room for paramedics and drivers.

Campus & Grounds

School Play Area — adjacent to the school building, easily supervised by staff, with a sports field for football and cricket, a half-court for basketball, playground equipment made from durable and safe materials, and shaded seating areas.

50-Bed Equipped Hospital — positioned with easy access for emergencies and its own ambulance entrance, featuring an Emergency Room, Outpatient Department, inpatient wards, operation theatres, a maternity ward, laboratory, pharmacy, imaging department, and staff accommodation.

Parking, Green Spaces & Walkways — ample, clearly marked parking near the main entrance, school, and hospital, including accessible parking spots; landscaped green areas with trees, shrubs, and flowering plants; and paved, well-lit walkways connecting every section of the complex.
