About Us

Khidmat-e-Khalq — Service to Humanity

Khwaja Gharib Nawaz Trust is a non-profit humanitarian welfare organization dedicated to serving humanity with compassion, dignity, and social responsibility.

Inspired by the values of service, generosity, and care for the underprivileged, the Trust works to uplift vulnerable communities through welfare and relief initiatives.

Our mission is centered on supporting individuals and families in need by addressing essential human needs such as food security, healthcare support, education assistance, and community welfare, while promoting values of harmony, compassion, and social well-being.

Alongside our humanitarian work, the Trust also preserves cultural and spiritual traditions as part of its community engagement activities. These initiatives are conducted with the aim of fostering unity, peace, and mutual respect within society.

Khwaja Gharib Nawaz Trust is a registered welfare organization committed to improving lives across Pakistan. Our work reflects the timeless principle of Khidmat-e-Khalq (service to humanity) by providing inclusive support without discrimination.

Our Vision

To build a society where every individual has access to basic necessities, lives with dignity, and receives support during times of hardship, while communities remain connected through shared values of compassion and harmony.

Our Mission

  • To serve humanity with sincerity, transparency, and accountability.
  • To provide sustainable welfare programs for underprivileged communities.
  • To promote social harmony and community well-being.
  • To support food security, healthcare, and education initiatives.
  • To encourage community engagement through ethical and responsible practices.
A generational trust

Our Noble Legacy & Leadership

Khwaja Gharib Nawaz Trust was established by a distinguished, well-educated, and noble family whose history is rooted in justice, spirituality, and selfless service to humanity. The Trust operates without any personal or commercial interest, serving as a continuation of generational welfare and moral values.

  1. Founder & Spiritual Patriarch

    Sajjada Nashin Hazrat Khwaja Moinuddin Khamis (R.A.)

    Nabira Khwaja Gharib Nawaz (R.A.) — the roots of this humanitarian mission trace back to this widely respected spiritual figure in Hyderabad, Sindh. Alongside spreading the message of the Chishti order, he initiated numerous public welfare efforts during his lifetime.

  2. Judicial, Legal & Spiritual Excellence

    Sajjada Nashin Syed Muhammad Ahteshamuddin Kazmi (R.A.)

    District & Sessions Judge, Nabira Khwaja Gharib Nawaz (R.A.) — this noble legacy was carried forward by his son. While holding a revered spiritual position in the Chishti order, he served the judiciary with utmost integrity as a District & Sessions Judge until his passing during active service.

  3. Legal Expertise & Generational Stewardship

    Sajjada Nashin Syed Amir Moin Kazmi (R.A.)

    Advocate High Court, Nabira Khwaja Gharib Nawaz (R.A.) — following his father's passing, his eldest son assumed the seat of Sajjadagi, preserving the family's dignity, legal acumen, and commitment to public welfare.

  4. Present Leadership

    Sajjada Nashin Pir Syed Adnan Mueen Kazmi

    Current Chairman, Nabira Khwaja Gharib Nawaz (R.A.) — today, the Trust and its spiritual heritage are led by his brother. As a prominent spiritual leader of the Chishti order in Hyderabad, he continues to guide the Trust's welfare initiatives with complete transparency, dedication, and integrity.

Programs

Our Work & Activities

Humanitarian Welfare Programs

  • Food Assistance: Daily rations, cooked meals, and community food distribution programs.
  • Health Support: Medical aid, health awareness, and emergency medical assistance.
  • Education Support: Assistance for orphans and deserving students.
  • Community Development: Clean drinking water initiatives and seasonal welfare campaigns.

Community & Cultural Engagement

As part of our community outreach, the Trust facilitates cultural and social gatherings aimed at promoting peace, unity, and social values. These activities are conducted alongside welfare efforts and include community meals and public participation initiatives that encourage inclusiveness and social cohesion.

Women Empowerment & Self-Reliance

Khwaja Gharib Nawaz Trust is dedicated to empowering women by fostering financial independence and self-reliance. We provide deserving women with the essential skill training, resources, and guidance needed to earn a dignified income from home or establish their own small businesses.

Why We Exist

We believe that supporting basic human needs is a shared responsibility. By focusing on welfare, compassion, and community support, Khwaja Gharib Nawaz Trust works to improve lives, strengthen communities, and provide hope to those in need.
