Our Projects

Building sustainable infrastructure for education, healthcare and community welfare

Khwajja Gharib Nawaz School (Grade I – X)

Quality education for underprivileged children with modern facilities and dedicated play areas

School Play Area

Location: Adjacent to the school building, easily supervised by staff.

Facilities:

  • Sports Field – medium-sized field for football, cricket and outdoor games
  • Basketball Court – half-court for basketball enthusiasts
  • Playground Equipment – swings, slides, climbing frames and seesaws for younger children
  • Shaded Seating Areas – for students to relax and socialize

50-Bed Equipped Hospital

A modern multi-story hospital designed to serve the community with essential medical infrastructure

Emergency Room (ER)

Fully equipped for medical emergencies with triage, examination rooms and resuscitation facilities.

Outpatient Department

Consulting rooms for General Medicine, Pediatrics, Gynaecology and other specialties.

Inpatient Wards

Male, female and pediatric wards with comfortable beds, nursing stations and patient monitoring.

Operation Theatres

Well-equipped operating theatre for surgical procedures.

Maternity & Lab

Safe childbirth facilities, diagnostic laboratory and pharmacy for essential medicines.

Imaging & Support

X-ray and ultrasound facilities, medical store, admin offices and waiting areas.

Parking, Green Spaces & Walkways

Thoughtfully designed complex with ample parking, landscaped green areas and accessible pathways

Parking: Ample space near main entrance, school and hospital with clearly marked bays including accessible spots and efficient traffic flow.

Green Spaces: Beautifully landscaped areas with trees, shrubs and flower beds throughout the complex.

Walkways: Safe, well-lit pedestrian paths connecting all facilities for students, patients and visitors.
