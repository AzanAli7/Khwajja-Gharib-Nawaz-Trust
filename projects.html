Our Projects

Future Projects of Pakistan

The Khwaja Gharib Nawaz Trust is dedicated to upholding the timeless values of humanitarian service. Our vision is to create a beacon of hope and progress, fostering a holistic environment where education empowers, faith enriches, healthcare heals, and compassion thrives.

We are committed to alleviating suffering, promoting knowledge, and providing essential resources to the most vulnerable members of society. Our work encompasses education, charity, medical supplies, and food provision.

Khwajja Garib Nawaz School (Grade I–X) & School Play Area

Location: Adjacent to the school building, easily supervised by staff.

  • Sports Field for football, cricket and other outdoor games.
  • Half-court basketball area.
  • Playground equipment for younger children.
  • Shaded seating areas.

50-Bed Equipped Hospital

A modern, multi-story hospital concept with essential medical infrastructure.

  • Emergency Room (ER)
  • Outpatient Department (OPD)
  • Inpatient wards
  • Operation Theatre
  • Maternity Ward
  • Laboratory
  • Pharmacy
  • Imaging Department
  • Medical store and equipment storage
  • Administrative and staff offices
  • Waiting areas
  • Optional staff accommodation

Parking Area, Green Spaces & Walkways

Ample parking near the main entrance, school and hospital, with accessible parking, efficient traffic flow, landscaped green areas, and paved, well-lit walkways.

Project Vision & Objectives — Pakistan

To establish a self-sustaining, integrated township that stands as a model for community development, providing state-of-the-art education, advanced healthcare, spiritual grounding, economic empowerment, and dignified living.

  • High-quality education from early childhood to vocational training.
  • Comprehensive and accessible healthcare.
  • Community and spiritual well-being.
  • Modern, comfortable and secure residential facilities for staff.
  • Sustainability through thoughtful infrastructure and green initiatives.

Strategic Zoning

  • Healthcare Complex: Hospital, Diagnostic Lab and Ambulance Bay.
  • Community & Commercial Core: Central Park, Sports Complex, Community Center, Market and Central Parking.
  • Educational and spiritual heart: Modern School of Technology, Central Big Library and Skills Development Institute.

The Modern School of Technology

Multi-story (4–5 floors), earthquake-resistant structure with natural light and ventilation; digital classrooms, smartboards, high-speed internet, video conferencing, STEM/Robotics Lab, advanced computer labs, 3D printing studio, language lab, auditorium, sports ground, basketball court, cafeteria and an O-Level wing.

Khwajja Gharib Nawaz Healthcare Complex

Khwajja Gharib Nawaz Charitable Hospital — 100 beds comprising general wards, private rooms and specialized units such as ICU and CCU.

Departments include 24/7 Emergency, General Medicine, Pediatrics, Gynecology, Orthopedics and Surgical Department. Equipment listed includes two operation theatres, ventilators, dialysis, X-Ray, Ultrasound and CT-Scan, with generator and solar backup.

Advanced Diagnostic Lab Building

Khwajja Gharib Nawaz Diagnostic Center with automated blood, pathology, microbiology and histopathology testing, plus digital X-Ray, Ultrasound and CT-Scan and digital reporting.

Advanced Ambulance Bay & Parking

Khwajja Gharib Nawaz Ambulance Service with capacity for 20 fully equipped ambulances: 5 ALS and 15 BLS, a maintenance workshop and staff rest room.
