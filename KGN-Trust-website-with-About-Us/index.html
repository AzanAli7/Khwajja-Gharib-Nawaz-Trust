WHO WE ARE

About Us

Khwaja Gharib Nawaz Trust is a non-profit humanitarian welfare organization dedicated to serving humanity with compassion, dignity, and social responsibility.

Our Purpose

Khwaja Gharib Nawaz Trust is a non-profit humanitarian welfare organization dedicated to serving humanity with compassion, dignity, and social responsibility. Inspired by the values of service, generosity, and care for the underprivileged, the Trust works to uplift vulnerable communities through welfare and relief initiatives.

Our mission is centered on supporting individuals and families in need by addressing essential human needs such as food security, healthcare support, education assistance, and community welfare, while promoting values of harmony, compassion, and social well-being.

Alongside our humanitarian work, the Trust also preserves cultural and spiritual traditions as part of its community engagement activities. These initiatives are conducted with the aim of fostering unity, peace, and mutual respect within society.

Khwaja Gharib Nawaz Trust is a registered welfare organization committed to improving lives across Pakistan. Our work reflects the timeless principle of Khidmat-e-Khalq (service to humanity) by providing inclusive support without discrimination.

OUR NOBLE LEGACY & LEADERSHIP

A tradition of service and stewardship

The Trust describes its legacy as rooted in justice, spirituality, education and selfless service to humanity.

01

Founder & Spiritual Patriarch

The roots of this humanitarian mission trace back to the family’s ancestor, Sajjada Nashin Hazrat Khwaja Moinuddin Khamis (R.A.) (Nabira Khwaja Gharib Nawaz R.A.), a widely respected spiritual figure in Hyderabad, Sindh. Alongside spreading the message of the Chishti order, he initiated numerous public welfare efforts during his lifetime.

02

Judicial, Legal & Spiritual Excellence

This noble legacy was carried forward by his son, Sajjada Nashin Syed Muhammad Ahteshamuddin Kazmi (R.A.) (District & Sessions Judge / Nabira Khwaja Gharib Nawaz R.A.). While holding a revered spiritual position in the Chishti order, he served the judiciary with utmost integrity as a District & Sessions Judge until his passing during active service.

03

Legal Expertise & Generational Stewardship

Following his father's passing, his eldest son, Sajjada Nashin Syed Amir Moin Kazmi (R.A.) (Advocate High Court / Nabira Khwaja Gharib Nawaz R.A.), assumed the seat of Sajjadagi, preserving the family’s dignity, legal acumen, and commitment to public welfare.

04

Present Leadership

Today, the Trust and its spiritual heritage are led by his brother, Sajjada Nashin Pir Syed Adnan Mueen Kazmi (Current Chairman / Nabira Khwaja Gharib Nawaz R.A.). As a prominent spiritual leader of the Chishti order in Hyderabad, he continues to guide the Trust's welfare initiatives with complete transparency, dedication, and integrity.

OUR WORK & ACTIVITIES

Where compassion becomes action

01

Humanitarian Welfare Programs

Food Assistance: Daily rations, cooked meals, and community food distribution programs.

Health Support: Medical aid, health awareness, and emergency medical assistance.

Education Support: Assistance for orphans and deserving students.

Community Development: Clean drinking water initiatives and seasonal welfare campaigns.

02

Community & Cultural Engagement

As part of our community outreach, the Trust facilitates cultural and social gatherings aimed at promoting peace, unity, and social values. These activities are conducted alongside welfare efforts and include community meals and public participation initiatives that encourage inclusiveness and social cohesion.

03

Women Empowerment & Self-Reliance

Khwaja Gharib Nawaz Trust is dedicated to empowering women by fostering financial independence and self-reliance. We provide deserving women with the essential skill training, resources, and guidance needed to earn a dignified income from home or establish their own small businesses.

Through tailored support and vocational initiatives, we enable women to become economically self-sufficient, support their families, and lead with dignity.
