Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

Khwajja Gharib Nawaz Trust respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect information when you visit our website or contact us.

Information We Collect

We may collect contact details you voluntarily provide (name, email, phone) when you inquire or donate. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for marketing.

Use of Information

Information is used solely to respond to inquiries, process donations, and improve our humanitarian services. Gallery images are used with respect for dignity and privacy of individuals shown.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data. Online banking for donations is handled by your bank; we do not store card details on this site.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, email info@kgntrust.org.
