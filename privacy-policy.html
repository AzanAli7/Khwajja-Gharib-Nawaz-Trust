3. How We Use Your Information

We process your Personal Data strictly for legitimate charitable and organizational purposes:

  1. Processing Donations: to process online donations, confirm receipt of funds, and issue official donation acknowledgments.
  2. Donor Communications: to send transaction receipts, project updates, annual impact reports, and updates regarding relief campaigns.
  3. Regulatory Compliance: to comply with Pakistani legal obligations, State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) regulations, anti-money laundering (AML) frameworks, and charitable trust record-keeping laws.
  4. Website Optimization & Security: to protect our website against fraud, unauthorized transactions, cyberattacks, and security breaches.

4. Payment and Financial Data Security (PCI-DSS Compliance)

KHWAJJA GHARIB NAWAZ TRUST prioritizes the absolute security of financial transactions made by our donors:

  • No Card Storage: We do not collect, process, or store credit/debit card numbers or bank account credentials on our website servers.
  • PCI-DSS Compliant Gateways: All online payments and donations are processed directly through licensed, authorized, and Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI-DSS) compliant third-party payment gateways.
  • Encryption: All communication between your browser and our website is protected using industry-standard Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) / Transport Layer Security (TLS) encryption technology.
  • Data Isolation: Donor financial identification details are strictly restricted to processing, verifying, and auditing donations, and preventing fraudulent transactions.
