About Us

Who We Are

KGN Trust works to improve the lives of vulnerable individuals and families by providing essential humanitarian support.

We focus on dignity, transparency, and compassion while serving communities regardless of religion, background, or ethnicity.

“Serving humanity through compassion, welfare, and social responsibility.”

Our Projects

Building a future of education, healthcare & community welfare

The project material below follows the content supplied from the Trust's project page.

Project Vision & Objectives

Integrated, self-sustaining community development

The project vision describes an integrated self-sustaining township combining high-quality education, vocational training, healthcare, community and spiritual wellbeing, staff housing, and sustainability/green initiatives. Project plan images have been removed from this version.

Education

Modern School of Technology

Planned facilities include digital classrooms, STEM/Robotics lab, advanced computer labs, 3D printing studio, language lab, auditorium, sports ground, basketball court, cafeteria and an O-Level wing.

Healthcare

Healthcare Complex

The supplied project content describes a charitable hospital with emergency services, OPD, wards, operating theaters, maternity, laboratory, pharmacy and imaging, with advanced diagnostic and critical-care facilities.

Diagnostics

Advanced Diagnostic Lab

Planned diagnostic services include automated blood, pathology, microbiology and histopathology, plus digital X-ray, ultrasound and CT with digital reporting.

Emergency Support

Advanced Ambulance Bay & Parking

The project material describes an ambulance service, parking for 20 ambulances, including 5 ALS and 15 BLS, plus workshop and staff facilities.
