KGN Trust is a non-profit humanitarian welfare organization dedicated to supporting underprivileged communities through food assistance, healthcare support, education, and social welfare initiatives.

Who We Are

KGN Trust works to improve the lives of vulnerable individuals and families by providing essential humanitarian support.

We focus on dignity, transparency, and compassion while serving communities regardless of religion, background, or ethnicity.

What We Do

  • Food Assistance & Community Feeding, Langer Programs
  • Medical Aid & Health Support Initiatives
  • Free Medical Camps & Basic Health Awareness
  • Support for Orphans and Widows
  • Education Assistance & School Support Programs
  • Emergency & Disaster Relief Activities
  • Winter Relief (Blankets, Clothing Distribution)
  • Clean Water & Basic Sanitation Support
  • Support for Underprivileged Families
  • Community Welfare & Social Support Services
